Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Tracue is a web app at app.tracue.com. It finds companies that show a reason to buy now, shows who to contact there, and lets you email them one at a time from your own mailbox. This page explains what data we have, where it comes from, what we do with it, and how long we keep it.

Tracue is run by IPAC Technology Company Limited, 6th Floor, 35 To Vinh Dien Street, Khuong Dinh Ward, Hanoi, Vietnam. Questions or requests: hello@tracue.com.

We do not sell personal data. Tracue is a tool for businesses and is not meant for children.

There are three groups of people whose data we handle:

  1. You, if you use Tracue (section 1–3).
  2. People who work at the companies in our results (section 4).
  3. People who receive an email sent through Tracue (section 5).

Tracue sends only the emails you press Send on, one at a time.

Replies (the Inbox). Tracue reads only the replies to emails you sent from Tracue, so you can see and answer them in Tracue. It does not open, list or keep any other email in your mailbox:

  • SMTP / IMAP mailboxes (including Gmail with an app password): for each email sent from Tracue in the last 30 days, Tracue asks your mail server only for messages that answer it (the reply headers name that email). Only those messages are downloaded. Tracue also looks for bounce notices about emails sent from Tracue.
  • Outlook and Microsoft 365: if you allow it when you connect (permission Mail.Read), Tracue looks up each email it sent and then reads only the messages of that same conversation.
  • Gmail connected with Sign in with Google is never read (see section 3). To see its replies in Tracue, add an app password.

When a reply says "please remove me" or "stop emailing me", Tracue puts that address on your do-not-contact list. To prepare your answer, when reply sorting is on, Tracue sends the text of the reply you received to our AI provider, Microsoft Azure OpenAI (see section 6), only to sort what the person asks. The AI does not write your answer and cannot block an address; without it, Tracue sorts the reply by its words alone. When you disconnect a mailbox, we delete the replies we kept from it.

3. Google user data

If you choose Sign in with Google, Tracue receives:

  • the email address of your Google account, so we know which mailbox the emails come from;
  • permission to send email from it (gmail.send).

Tracue cannot read, store or change the emails in your Gmail. We use this data only to send the emails you write and press Send on in Tracue. The key is encrypted when stored; when you disconnect, we delete it and revoke it with Google.

We do not sell or share Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models. Nobody at Tracue reads it unless you ask us to, it is needed for security, or the law requires it. Your Gmail address is not sent to any AI provider.

Tracue's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To remove access: press Disconnect in Email › Mailboxes, or remove Tracue on your Google Account's permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Microsoft accounts: Tracue asks for permission to send (Mail.Send), to stay connected, for your address, and to read mail (Mail.Read). Microsoft offers no narrower permission, so Tracue limits itself: it reads only the replies to emails sent from Tracue, as described in section 2, and never lists or keeps other emails. The key is encrypted when stored; when you disconnect, we delete it, revoke it with Microsoft, and delete the replies we kept.

5. If you received an email sent through Tracue

  • A Tracue user wrote it and sent it from their own mailbox. They are the sender and are responsible for what it says. Their name and postal address are at the bottom.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click, no account, no form. It stops that user from emailing you through Tracue again, straight away. The link works for at least a year.
  • If you are in the EU or the UK, the email also says where your work email was found and that you can object at any time. From the unsubscribe page you can also ask us to remove your details from Tracue.
  • Open tracking is never added to emails sent to people in the EU or the UK.
  • If you reply, your reply is kept in that user's Tracue account so they can see and answer it (section 2). If you ask to be removed, your address goes on their do-not-contact list.

7. Your choices

  • Change or remove what you entered at any time in the app.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account, in Settings › Privacy & data or at hello@tracue.com. For now our team deletes accounts by hand: within one month of your request we delete your account and everything in it, and backups are overwritten within about 5 weeks after that. We keep only the request itself, as described in section 1, and two scrambled records that can't be turned back into email addresses: people who asked not to be emailed, so they stay blocked (we must respect their request), and your email address for 12 months, so starting credits are not given twice to the same person.
  • If you are in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.
