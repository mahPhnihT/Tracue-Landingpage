Under the hood

A data engine built for accuracy, not volume.

Most data vendors sell you a big number. We'd rather sell you a true one. Every record flows through the same pipeline before it reaches your screen.

Collect

Public web, registries and partner sources, gathered responsibly.

Clean

De-duplicate, normalise and resolve entities from raw to clean.

Verify

Domain and mailbox checks with a confidence score per record.

Score

Match each account to your ICP and rank by buying signals.

Deliver

App, CSV, CRM or API — wherever your team works.

Accuracy
Verified or flagged. Never guessed.

Each contact carries a status and a confidence score, so you decide what's safe to send.

  • Catch-all & disposable detection
  • Role vs. personal inbox tagging
  • Re-verification on a rolling schedule

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Tracue?+

Tracue is a go-to-market intelligence platform. It helps companies that build products find the businesses and decision-makers who need what they've built, verify how to reach them, and start the conversation — all in one place.

When can I use it?+

We started building in August 2026. A private alpha opens to design partners in December 2026, closed beta follows in early 2027, and v1.0 launches publicly in March 2027. Join the waitlist to get in early.

How much will it cost?+

There will be a free tier, then usage-based plans — you pay for verified data you actually use, not for seats. Founding members get 50% off their first year, locked in.

Where does the data come from?+

From publicly available sources, business registries and partner datasets, processed through our own clean-and-verify pipeline. We're transparent about sourcing and honor opt-out requests.

Is it compliant with privacy laws?+

Privacy is a design principle for us, not an afterthought. Tracue is being built around GDPR and CCPA principles, with suppression lists, opt-out handling and clear data provenance.

Can I become a design partner?+

Yes — that's exactly what the founding member program is for. Leave your email below and tell us a little about your product. We'll reach out personally.
